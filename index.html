          Complete App Reference

          📋 What is ProTrack?

          ProTrack is a customizable project accountability system (31, 25, 20, 15, or 10 days). Each day you log your work, earn XP, and push your project through 4 intelligently scaled milestones: Chassis Done (30%) → Electronics Done (60%) → Code Done (80%) → Project Complete (100%). Your Advisor evolves alongside your progress, unlocking new visual stages as you advance.

          1. Daily Logging

          Navigate to the + tab to submit your daily work entry. You must provide at least 30 characters of description, a photo of your work, and optionally log milestone count and hours. Richer logs earn more progress and XP. Only one log is allowed per calendar day — the form locks after submission.

          2. Milestones & Hours Dedicated

          Milestones Accomplished are specific, measurable tasks completed that day (e.g., "3 parts printed", "motor code tested"). These are the primary driver of project progress. Hours Dedicated records your total active work time — fill it manually or use the Focus Clock to auto-capture it. Hours directly affect your daily XP multiplier and your overall project rating.

          3. Focus Clock & Pomodoro

          Tap Clock In on the dashboard to start tracking your active session time. It auto-fills the Hours Dedicated field when you Clock Out. The Pomodoro timer runs 25-minute focused work intervals followed by 5-minute breaks to maximize your concentration. Starting the Focus Clock also auto-starts the Pomodoro.

          4. Star-Chart Timeline

          Found in the Nexus Labs (⚙ tab). Each row represents one of your 4 major project milestones. Lime-green cells = days where you have already achieved that milestone. Faint green cells = the expected pace for that goal based on the 31-day schedule. Blue-bordered cells = the exact target deadline day for each milestone. If your lime trail is shorter than the faint green trail, you are behind schedule and your Advisor will alert you.

          5. Peer Review Gate

          After completing each major phase (30%, 60%, 80%, 100%), a Peer Review must be passed before progress can continue. Go to the ✓ tab, rate each criterion honestly using the score buttons, and achieve a minimum of 80% to unlock the next phase. Scoring below 80% means you must refine your weakest areas and try again. Passing on time earns a 125 XP bonus.

          6. Nexus Labs & BOM

          The Nexus Labs tab houses your Star-Chart Timeline and your Component Inventory (Bill of Materials). Add every part you need, its cost, and procurement status (Ordered, In Stock, etc.). The total investment is automatically calculated so you always know your running project budget.

          7. SWOT & Project Advisor

          The SWOT tab auto-generates a Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats analysis from your live project data — your streak, current pace vs. expected, and recent blockers. Your Project Advisor on the dashboard delivers context-aware daily messages: motivational when on track, tactical when lagging, and strategic when ahead of schedule.

          8. Nexus Market

          Tap the 🛍 icon on the dashboard to open the Nexus Market. Spend your earned XP to unlock interface themes (color palettes) and Advisor accessories — hats, shades, emblems, ribbons, and more. Applied accessories are permanently saved and visible on your dashboard. Enjoy your free Sport Cap to sample customization! You can now toggle accessories on and off with the equip/unequip button.

          9. Relics & XP

          XP is earned by submitting daily logs and is your currency in the Nexus Market. Relics are hidden achievement badges — unlock them by hitting special milestones: logging at midnight, achieving a 10-day streak, adding 15+ inventory items, completing Objective 1 in under 5 days, and earning a perfect Peer Review score. Tap your Advisor avatar to open the Relic Vault and inspect your collection.

          10. Offline Mode & PWA

          ProTrack works 100% offline. All your data is stored locally on your device — no accounts, no cloud required. Install it as a Progressive Web App (PWA) by tapping "Add to Home Screen" in your browser to get a full app experience. Your data persists between sessions automatically.
